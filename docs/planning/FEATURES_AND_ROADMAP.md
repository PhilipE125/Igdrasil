# Igdrasil — Features & Roadmap

> Source of truth for landing-page copy. Everything here is framed as a benefit a Swedish founder, freelancer, or small team would actually experience — not the implementation behind it.

---

## 1. What Igdrasil does today

### Get receipts and invoices in — any way you want
- **Drag and drop** any PDF, JPEG, or photo of a receipt into the app.
- **Snap a photo on the go** — send it straight to your private Telegram bot.
- **Connect Gmail** to back-fill invoices from your inbox in one click.
- **No double-entry, ever** — duplicates are caught automatically.

### Bookkeeping on autopilot
- **Reads your invoices for you** — supplier, amounts, VAT, dates, line items, all extracted without typing.
- **Books to the right account** — every line is mapped to the Swedish BAS chart of accounts automatically.
- **Currency-aware** — invoices in EUR, USD, anything — converted at the right Riksbanken rate, to the ore.
- **Card and expense rules** — receipts paid with the company card are classified the way you want.
- **Recognises your suppliers** — once Igdrasil has seen a supplier, it remembers how to book them.

### Sweden-ready compliance, filed for you
- **Momsdeklaration (VAT)** prepared automatically — standard 25%, reduced 12 % / 6 %, EU reverse charge, exports.
- **Employer declarations (AGI)** with wage period tracking, ready to file.
- **INK2 and NE-bilaga** for small business income reporting.
- **Year-end closing** — closing entries and balance rollover handled.
- **Audit trail** — every change is logged and tamper-evident, ready for a revisor.

### A real ledger, not a black box
- **Double-entry bookkeeping** with full debit/credit balancing.
- **Trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow** — generated on demand.
- **Cost centers and projects** on every line, so you can see where the money actually went.
- **Fiscal periods** with proper open / closed / locked states.

### Get paid faster
- **Send customer invoices** by email — drafted, issued, paid, credited.
- **Track payments and reminders** with aging built in.
- **Credit notes** generated and tracked end-to-end.
- **Multi-currency invoicing** with the right FX on every line.

### Pay suppliers without the admin
- **Supplier register** with org/VAT numbers, payment terms, bank details.
- **Payment queue** — see what's due, schedule payments, mark them paid.
- **Bankgiro, PlusGiro, IBAN** all supported.

### Connect your bank in minutes
- **Statement import** for Juni, Lunar, Nordea, SEB, Swedbank, Handelsbanken, and Skatteverket.
- **Auto-match** every bank transaction to an invoice or supplier.
- **Visual reconciliation** — see at a glance what's matched, what's outstanding.

### Time tracking that turns into invoices
- **One-tap timer** — start, stop, pause, project.
- **Type it like you'd say it** — "2h project A, 1.5h project B".
- **Auto-invoice billable hours** — push them into a customer invoice.

### Works with what you already use
- **Fortnox** — full two-way sync (suppliers, accounts, projects, fiscal years, vouchers, attachments, customers, articles, contracts, assets). Bureau (byra) mode supported.
- **Visma eAccounting** — connect and sync.
- **Bjorn Lundén, Briox, Bokio** — all supported.
- **SIE-4** import and export — bring your history with you, leave whenever you want.
- **Gmail and Telegram** for inbound documents.

### Ask Igdrasil anything
- **Plain-language chat** about your books — "How much VAT do I owe this quarter?", "Which clients are late?", "What did I spend on travel in March?".
- **Charts, tables, KPIs and audit trails** generated live as part of the answer.
- **Backed by Skatteverket guidance** — answers grounded in current Swedish tax docs.
- **Choose your model** — Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini, your call.

### Your dashboard, in real time
- **Live KPIs** — net profit, revenue, expenses, document counts, trends.
- **Activity feed** that updates the moment something changes.
- **Reports tab** with everything from trial balance to regulatory filings.
- **File archive** to find any source document in seconds.

### Built for teams
- **Invite teammates** with their own logins.
- **Multi-company** — agencies and bureaus can run several businesses from one account.
- **Your data, isolated** — strict per-company separation enforced at the database level.

## 2. What's coming next

### Forward any invoice to a private Igdrasil inbox
Send or auto-forward bills to `you@inbox.igdrasil.se` and they appear in your books — no upload, no app, no friction. Spam and duplicates filtered out automatically.

### An assistant that takes action — not just chat
The next version of the assistant doesn't just answer. Ask it to *post a journal entry, approve an invoice, sync to Fortnox, create a supplier, run a month-end close* — and it does it, with an approval card before anything touches your ledger.

### Pre-built workflows ("recipes")
One-click runbooks for the things every founder dreads:
- **Month-end close**
- **VAT reconciliation**
- **Fortnox sync troubleshooting**

### Search across your own documents
Ask questions across every contract, receipt, and report you've ever uploaded — "What's in our office lease?", "How much did we spend with this vendor last year?".

### Web research, built in
The assistant can browse the web when it needs to — checking a vendor's org number, looking up a tax rule, verifying an exchange rate.

### Mobile
A mobile app to snap, approve, and check the books from your phone — currently desktop-first.

### More integrations
A growing roster of bank, billing, and ERP connectors, on the same two-way sync foundation that powers Fortnox today.

## 3. The promise

- **Built for Swedish founders.** Bokforingslagen, Skatteverket, BAS, SIE-4 — all native, not bolted on.
- **Hands-on when it matters, automated when it doesn't.** A real bookkeeper behind the software for the edge cases.
- **Pricing without surprises.** Flat monthly fee plus a small markup on the AI compute we actually use on your books. No per-seat fees. No year-end "true-ups".
- **Your data, exportable.** SIE-4 in, SIE-4 out. Leave whenever you want.
