# Igdrasil Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Effective from:** 2026-08-23
**Version:** 1.0

This policy explains which cookies and similar browser storage Igdrasil uses on the website at `igdrasil.se` and in the Service at `accounting.igdrasil.se`, what each one is for, and how you control them. It supplements our [privacy notice](/privacy/), which describes how we handle personal data more broadly.

## 1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. Related technologies, such as `localStorage`, work the same way from a privacy point of view: they keep a small amount of data in your browser between page loads. Where this policy says "cookie" it covers both.

## 2. This website sets no cookies

`igdrasil.se` sets no cookies at all. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no third-party script on it. Fonts are served from our own domain rather than from a font provider, so loading a page here does not tell anyone else that you visited.

One thing is stored in your browser, and it never leaves your device:

| Item | Storage | Purpose | Category | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `theme` | `localStorage` | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark appearance, so the page does not flash the wrong one on your next visit | Functional preference | Until you clear your browser's site data |

Because nothing here is used for analytics or advertising, there is no consent banner to click through. If that changes, this page will list the new cookies before they are set, and we will ask for consent first.

## 3. Cookies in the Service

The Service at `accounting.igdrasil.se` is a signed-in application and needs cookies to work.

| Cookie | Purpose | Category | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in and session cookies set by our authentication provider | Keeps you signed in, ties a request to your account, and protects the session against tampering | Strictly necessary | For as long as you remain signed in |

Strictly necessary cookies are set without asking, because the Service cannot work without them. This is permitted under the Electronic Communications Act (2022:482) and does not require consent.

Product analytics inside the Service records how features are used, so that we can see where people get stuck. It can be switched off per user under account settings.

## 4. What we do not do

We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of you across other websites, we do not sell data collected through cookies, and we do not use cookie data to make automated decisions about you.

## 5. How to control what is stored

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing site data for `igdrasil.se` removes the stored appearance preference. Blocking cookies for `accounting.igdrasil.se` will stop you from signing in to the Service.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the cookies we use change. The current version is always published at `https://igdrasil.se/cookies/`. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.

## 7. Contact

Questions about cookies, or about how we handle your data: **privacy@igdrasil.se**.
