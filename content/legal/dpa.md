# Igdrasil Data Processing Agreement

**Last updated:** 2026-08-23
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** All paid and trial use of the Igdrasil platform

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the [general terms and conditions](/terms/) between Igdrasil AB, org. nr 559570-1011, Kornhamnstorg 61, 111 27 Stockholm, Sweden ("Igdrasil", "Processor") and the customer that has accepted those terms ("Customer", "Controller"). It records the terms required by Article 28(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

This is the current standard form. It applies automatically to every customer, with no signature required. A countersigned copy is available on request at privacy@igdrasil.se.

## 1. Roles of the parties

The Customer is the controller of the personal data contained in Customer Data. Igdrasil is the processor and processes that personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions.

Igdrasil acts as an independent controller for a limited set of processing that it decides on itself: account administration, billing, security logging, service communications, and the improvement of the Service using aggregated data that no longer identifies any individual. That processing is described in the [privacy notice](/privacy/) and is outside the scope of this DPA.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

The subject matter is the provision of the Igdrasil accounting platform as described in the general terms. Processing continues for as long as the Customer's subscription is active, and afterwards only as set out in section 11.

## 3. Nature and purpose of the processing

Igdrasil processes personal data in order to:

- receive, store and organise accounting documents, receipts, invoices and bank transaction data;
- extract structured information from documents, including by means of the AI features described in section 7 of the privacy notice;
- suggest, record and reconcile accounting entries;
- generate reports, filings and exports at the Customer's request;
- retain records for the periods required by the Swedish Bookkeeping Act (2018:1125) and other applicable law;
- provide support to the Customer's users.

## 4. Categories of data subjects

- The Customer's employees, board members and other authorised users of the Service
- The Customer's customers, suppliers and other counterparties appearing in accounting records
- Individuals named in documents the Customer or its users upload

## 5. Categories of personal data

- Identity and contact data: name, email address, telephone number, postal address, organisation or personal identity number where it appears in a document
- Account data: user identifier, role, sign-in metadata
- Financial and transaction data: bank account numbers, transaction narratives, amounts, invoice and receipt content, payroll totals where posted to the ledger
- Content of documents uploaded to or fetched into the Service
- Support correspondence

No special categories of personal data under GDPR Art. 9 are requested by the Service. If the Customer nonetheless uploads documents containing such data, the Customer remains responsible for having a lawful basis under Art. 9(2).

## 6. Instructions

Igdrasil processes personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which consist of this DPA, the general terms, and the Customer's use of the Service and its configuration options. Igdrasil will inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection law.

Where Igdrasil is required by Union or Member State law to process personal data beyond those instructions, it will inform the Customer of that requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it on important grounds of public interest.

## 7. Confidentiality

Igdrasil ensures that every person authorised to process personal data under this DPA is bound by a duty of confidentiality, whether contractual or statutory, and that access is limited to those who need it to perform their duties.

## 8. Security

Igdrasil implements the technical and organisational measures required by GDPR Art. 32. The current measures are described on the [security and infrastructure overview](/security-overview/), which constitutes Annex II to this DPA. Igdrasil may update those measures over time, provided the level of security is not reduced.

## 9. Sub-processors

The Customer gives a general authorisation for Igdrasil to engage sub-processors. The current list is published at [Igdrasil sub-processors](/subprocessors/), which constitutes Annex III to this DPA.

Igdrasil will give the Customer reasonable advance notice, by email or in-product message, before a new sub-processor starts processing Customer Data. The Customer may object on reasonable grounds relating to data protection within 30 days of that notice. If the parties cannot agree on a solution, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the subscription without penalty.

Igdrasil imposes on each sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains fully liable to the Customer for the performance of each sub-processor's obligations.

## 10. International transfers

Igdrasil's own production environment is located in the European Union. Where a sub-processor processes personal data outside the EU/EEA, the transfer is made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), Module Two (controller to processor) or Module Three (processor to processor) as applicable, incorporated into this DPA by reference and supplemented by the technical measures described in section 8.

For the purposes of the Standard Contractual Clauses: the optional docking clause applies; the supervisory authority is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY); the governing law is Swedish law; and the forum is the courts of Sweden. Annexes I, II and III to the Clauses are populated by sections 2 to 5 of this DPA, the security overview, and the sub-processor list respectively.

## 11. Deletion and return

On termination of the subscription, Igdrasil will, at the Customer's choice, return the Customer Data in a commonly used machine-readable format or delete it. Export functions are available in the Service throughout the subscription and during any wind-down period agreed in the general terms.

Igdrasil will retain personal data after termination only where Union or Member State law requires it, in particular accounting records that must be kept under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act. Retained data stays subject to the security measures in section 8 and is not processed for any other purpose.

Backups are deleted on their ordinary rotation schedule rather than individually purged. Until a backup expires, restored data remains subject to this DPA.

## 12. Assistance to the Controller

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Igdrasil will assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, with:

- responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under GDPR Chapter III;
- meeting the obligations in GDPR Art. 32 to 36, including security of processing, breach notification and data protection impact assessments;
- prior consultation with a supervisory authority where required.

If a data subject contacts Igdrasil directly about Customer Data, Igdrasil will not respond on the substance but will refer the request to the Customer without undue delay.

## 13. Personal data breach

Igdrasil will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data. The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point for further information. Where the full information is not available at once, it will be provided in phases as it becomes available.

## 14. Audit

Igdrasil will make available to the Customer the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with GDPR Art. 28 and will allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates.

In the first instance Igdrasil will respond to a written audit questionnaire and provide available documentation of its security measures. An on-site inspection may be requested no more than once per calendar year, on at least 30 days' written notice, during normal business hours, subject to confidentiality undertakings, and in a way that does not disrupt the Service or compromise the data of other customers. Igdrasil may charge its reasonable costs for an on-site inspection.

## 15. Liability and precedence

The liability provisions of the general terms apply to this DPA. Where this DPA conflicts with the general terms in respect of the processing of personal data, this DPA prevails. Where this DPA conflicts with the Standard Contractual Clauses, the Clauses prevail.

## 16. Term and changes

This DPA takes effect when the Customer accepts the general terms and remains in force for as long as Igdrasil processes personal data on the Customer's behalf.

Igdrasil may update this DPA to reflect changes in law, in the Service, or in its sub-processors. Material changes are notified to active customers at least 30 days in advance. The current version is always published at `https://igdrasil.se/dpa/`.
