## 1. Scope

For Igdrasil it is important that you feel comfortable with how we process your personal data. This privacy notice describes how Igdrasil processes personal data relating to customers, their representatives, users of the service, suppliers and partners, website visitors, and other data subjects whose personal data we process.

Igdrasil offers a complete accounting automation platform that connects people, companies and organizations (the "**Platform**"). Through the Platform our customers have access to the products and services Igdrasil makes available from time to time, designed to make the lives of business operators easier by offering smart services within bookkeeping, document management, bank reconciliation, reporting, and integrations with third-party services. The bookkeeping services and other services made available via the Platform are collectively referred to as the "**Services**". The Platform also offers integrations provided by external partners ("**Integration Partners**").

## 6. Profiling and Automated Decision-Making

### 6.1 Profiling

Igdrasil uses, in certain cases, what is known as profiling. This means automatic processing of personal data to perform analyses about our customers' economic situation, together with users' personal choices or behaviour on the Platform. Profiling is used, for example, to analyse and develop the Services or in connection with marketing.

We use profiling in the processing activities described above in section 5, for example for:

- Collection and analysis of user behaviour for product development and product testing.
- Segmentation of profiles for marketing efforts.
- Risk classification of customers prior to entering into a contract and during the contractual relationship.

#### 6.1.1 Specifically about profiling for marketing

When you interact with Igdrasil, for example by visiting Igdrasil's web pages or using Igdrasil applications, attending events and webinars, or using the Platform and the Services, Igdrasil will process personal data on the basis of a legitimate interest in processing personal data for marketing to you as a customer or prospective customer. Alternatively we base the processing on your consent, in cases where we use more advanced profiling of you as a customer or prospective customer.

By analyzing information about how you use our Services and how you interact with us, we can adapt the marketing and recommendations you receive from us. As an example of profiling we may use look-alike profiles of existing customers of a product (i.e., profiles that share the same characteristics and traits as another profile) and then market the product to you who have been identified as fitting a look-alike profile. As an existing user you may see the marketing inside the Service while logged in; as a prospective customer you may see this for example as ads on other websites.

### 6.2 Automated Decision-Making

Igdrasil does not currently make decisions concerning you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you or that significantly affect you in a similar way (GDPR Art. 22). Where the Service produces account suggestions, transaction-matching suggestions, or extraction results, these are presented to a user for review and approval; the human user is the decision-maker.

## 7. Use of AI

Igdrasil uses artificial intelligence (AI) within the Services to make day-to-day work faster and more accurate. Examples include extraction of accounting-relevant information from receipts, invoices and other documents; suggesting which BAS account a transaction or invoice should be posted to; and an in-app assistant that helps users navigate and use the Service.

**How AI is provided.** The Service calls large language models operated by third-party AI providers over their APIs; which provider handles a given task is a configuration choice. Where the Service is configured to use an EU-hosted inference service, it accepts only EU-scoped model routes, a route that is not EU-scoped is rejected in code before any request leaves our systems. Section 8.4 explains how to obtain the current list of providers, and section 10 covers processing outside the EU/EEA.

Where AI is used, we apply the following principles:

- **Human review**: AI output is presented as suggestions. Proposed bookkeeping changes are created as staged operations that a user reviews and approves; the Service does not post to the books on the strength of a model output alone.
- **No training on customer data**: we engage AI providers under business API terms, under which data submitted through the API is not used to train their general-purpose models. We do not otherwise make customer data available for model training.
- **Purpose limitation**: data sent to AI services is limited to what is needed to perform the specific task and is not reused for unrelated purposes. Workflow and integration payloads reject fields that look like credentials or secrets before they are passed on.
- **Opt out**: a customer can ask us to switch off AI-assisted extraction for its account, in which case documents are entered manually. We action this request manually today; a self-service setting is planned.

Some processing takes place outside the EU/EEA:

Where a transfer outside the EU/EEA occurs we ensure that it takes place subject to appropriate safeguards under the GDPR, in particular:

1. An **adequacy decision** by the European Commission (Art. 45);
2. **Standard Contractual Clauses** approved by the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c)), supplemented with technical and organizational measures (such as encryption and pseudonymization where feasible); or
3. Your **explicit consent** (Art. 49(1)(a)), in clearly delimited and occasional cases.

## 11. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Under the GDPR you have a number of rights in relation to the personal data we process about you. You can exercise your rights at any time by contacting us as described in section 15.

- **Right of access** (Art. 15), you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16), you may have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected.
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17), you may have your personal data deleted, subject to legal retention obligations (such as the seven-year retention requirement for bookkeeping records under the Bookkeeping Act, which we cannot waive).

- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20), where processing is based on contract or consent and is carried out by automated means, you may receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
- **Right to object** (Art. 21), to processing based on a legitimate interest, including profiling for direct marketing.
- **Right to withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3)), where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
- **Right to lodge a complaint** with the Swedish supervisory authority **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**: `imy.se`, +46 8 657 61 00, postal: Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm.

We respond to verified rights requests within 30 days of receipt, with the possibility of a 60-day extension for complex requests as permitted by Art. 12(3).

## 12. Technical and Organizational Security Measures

The measures below describe what is implemented in the Service today.

- **Encryption in transit.** Public traffic is served over HTTPS with TLS 1.2 as the minimum protocol version. Production database connections verify the server certificate and hostname.
- **Encryption at rest.** The production database and its disk volumes are encrypted with AES-256 under a customer-managed key in AWS KMS, and document storage is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Credentials for connected third-party services are additionally sealed in AES-256-GCM envelopes under versioned keys.
- **Access control.** Every API request is authenticated and resolved to a user and a company, and access to a company's data requires an active membership of that company. Row-level security policies are defined on tenant tables in the database. Multi-factor authentication is available through our authentication provider.
- **Audit trail.** Security-relevant actions are written to an audit log in which each entry carries the hash of its predecessor, together with an endpoint that verifies the chain and so detects tampering.
- **Rate limiting** on bank, email-ingestion and integration endpoints.
- **Backups.** The production database is backed up automatically, with point-in-time recovery, and the backups are encrypted with the same key as the database.
- **Secret hygiene.** Database URLs and secret-like fields are redacted from logs, and workflow and integration payloads reject fields that look like credentials.
- **Personnel.** Access to production systems is granted on a need-to-know basis and is covered by confidentiality undertakings.
- **Incidents.** If a personal-data breach occurs we assess it and, where the GDPR requires it, notify Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten within 72 hours and affected data subjects without undue delay (Art. 33–34).
- **Providers.** Written data-processing agreements with every provider that processes personal data on our behalf.

Igdrasil is not currently certified under ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2. We are building toward a management system that can be certified, and we describe our controls as they are implemented rather than as they are planned.

## 13. Cookies

**On this website we set no cookies.** `igdrasil.se` carries no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking script, so there is nothing to consent to. Your browser stores one preference locally, the light or dark appearance you chose, and it never leaves your device.

**In the Service** (`accounting.igdrasil.se`) sign-in and session cookies are set by our authentication provider. They are strictly necessary: without them you cannot stay signed in. Product analytics inside the Service can be switched off per user under account settings.

We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of website visitors across other websites.
