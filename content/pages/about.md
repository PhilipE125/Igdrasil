## Our story

Igdrasil did not begin with the idea of building another accounting system. It began with a shared conviction: business owners deserve better tools to understand and run their finances.

We, Edith Wolff and Philip Eriksson, met in the BSc programme in Business & Economics at the Stockholm School of Economics. We quickly became a couple and have worked closely side by side ever since.

We wrote our bachelor's thesis together in accounting and received the **Per Hiller Prize for the best thesis in Accounting, Valuation & Financial Management**. We then continued with master's studies in the same field and also completed CEMS. We wrote our master's thesis together as well and graduated with the highest grade.

It was a formative period. It gave us a strong foundation in accounting, valuation and financial management, but also an insight that became central to Igdrasil: bookkeeping is far more than a legal requirement. When handled well, it is one of a business's most important sources of information.

## From the Stockholm School of Economics to Igdrasil

During our studies, we saw financial work from different perspectives.

Philip worked as a fractional CFO. There, he saw how much time is spent when documents are missing, payments need reconciling and the same questions return every month. He began using AI to produce exception reports faster and identify patterns that are easy to miss in a manual review.

Edith worked in go-to-market at a fast-growing company and at SSE Business Lab, close to entrepreneurs and businesses at different stages of growth. It became clear how often finance falls behind the business: the data exists, but it is scattered and difficult to use when important decisions need to be made.

We saw the same pattern again and again: business owners have plenty of information, but too little time and too few tools to make it coherent and useful.

That became the starting point for Igdrasil.

## Two perspectives on the same problem

When we began building Igdrasil, we wanted to solve both the practical and the strategic problem in a company's finances.

The practical problem is familiar: receipts, invoices, bank events and reports arrive from many places and must be handled correctly. The strategic problem is just as important: once the bookkeeping is up to date, it should be able to answer questions about cash flow, costs, margins, VAT and what is actually happening in the business.

We do not believe the answer is to shift more responsibility onto the business owner or hide the bookkeeping in a black box. It should be easier to collect, safer to process and easier to understand. The personal relationship between a business owner and their accounting firm should have more room, not less, when less time is spent on repetitive administration.

## Why we are building Igdrasil

Our vision is simple: **bookkeeping should help you make better decisions.**

For us, that means financial information where documents and transactions are connected, proposals can be reviewed before posting and users can get answers while the question still matters. Modern AI can automate work that otherwise takes unnecessary time, but accounting also requires control, traceability and clear boundaries.

That is why Igdrasil combines automation with accounting-specific **guardrails**. AI agents should work from real supporting documentation, the business's accounting logic and clear permissions. Uncertain or unusual cases should be surfaced for human review, and every accounting proposal is approved by a person before it is posted.

We are building Igdrasil so business owners and accounting firms have more time for the work that requires judgement, relationships and real business decisions.

## What we are building

Igdrasil is a Swedish AI-powered accounting platform for bookkeeping, invoicing, payroll, VAT, reporting, budgeting, forecasting and other business insights. It is built for Swedish businesses and accounting firms, with Swedish accounting logic, double-entry bookkeeping, the BAS chart of accounts and audit trails at its core.

In practice, Igdrasil helps to:

- collect documents and information from the workflows where they originate,
- read receipts and invoices and suggest account coding and VAT treatment,
- match bank events with invoices and identify likely duplicates,
- provide a clearer view of cash flow, receivables, costs and results,
- let users connect the AI chat or model that suits them through Igdrasil's CLI, with controlled access to their financial data.

We want users to be able to follow their data, understand their figures and take their information with them when needed. That is why Igdrasil offers free exports in SIE4 and raw format, including supporting documentation, at any time.

## Frequently asked questions about Igdrasil

### Who founded Igdrasil?

Igdrasil was founded by Edith Wolff and Philip Eriksson in early 2026. They met while studying Business & Economics at the Stockholm School of Economics and are the co-founders and owners of the company.

### What does Igdrasil do?

Igdrasil is building a Swedish AI-powered platform for bookkeeping, invoicing, payroll, VAT, reporting, budgeting, forecasting and business insights. The platform is made for Swedish businesses and accounting firms.

### Why was Igdrasil founded?

Igdrasil was founded to make bookkeeping less administrative and more useful. The goal is to bring financial data together, automate repetitive work with appropriate controls and help businesses make better-informed decisions.

### Is Igdrasil built for accounting firms or businesses?

Both. Igdrasil helps accounting and bookkeeping firms work more efficiently with their client businesses, while business owners and finance leaders can gain a clearer view of their own finances.

### How does Igdrasil use AI in bookkeeping?

AI can read supporting documentation, suggest account coding, match bank events and help analyse financial data. Igdrasil's guardrails, permission controls and audit trails constrain how AI agents can work. Accounting proposals are reviewed and approved by a person before posting.
